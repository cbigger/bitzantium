The Aether is a vast, formless expanse — neither light nor dark, but something in between. The air hums with a low, resonant tone, as if the space itself is vibrating at a frequency just below hearing. There is no ground in the traditional sense, only a shimmering surface that feels solid underfoot but ripples faintly with each step, like walking on still water.

Distant shapes drift at the edges of perception — fragments of places, half-formed structures that dissolve when looked at directly. The space feels transitional, a threshold between somewhere and nowhere. It is quiet here, but not silent. The hum carries whispers — too faint to make out, but unmistakably present.

Nothing hostile stirs. The Aether is patient. It waits.
